Case Study

Anytime Fitness

Marketing advisory and social media strategy for a growing franchise network.

Client

Anytime Fitness

Scope

Advisory & Strategy

Services

Social Media Audit, Marketing Strategy

Platform Focus

Instagram

The Situation

Posting a lot.
Landing nothing.

The team were putting effort into their social media. Posting regularly, trying different content, doing what they thought was right. But nothing was sticking. Engagement was flat, reach was limited, and the content wasn't converting followers into foot traffic.

The problem wasn't effort. It was strategy. They didn't have a clear framework for what to post, when to post it, or how to make their content stand out in a space full of generic gym imagery.

The Advisory

The right advice
at the right time.

We audited their existing content and identified what was working, what was noise, and where the real opportunities were. From there, we provided ongoing strategic guidance on content direction, posting cadence, and how to position their brand locally on Instagram.

The focus was practical, actionable advice. What types of content to create, how to structure posts for engagement, when to post for maximum reach, and how to tell stories that make people want to walk through the door.

Their team executed the work themselves. We provided the strategy and the framework. No hand-holding, just clear direction they could run with.

What We Covered

01

Social Media Audit

Full review of existing content, identifying what was working, what was wasted effort, and where the gaps were.

02

Content Strategy

Clear framework for what to post, when, and why. Built around content pillars that resonate with a local fitness audience.

03

Instagram Growth

Platform-specific guidance on Stories, Reels, feed posts, and engagement tactics to grow a real local following.

04

Marketing Strategy

Broader advice beyond social, including positioning, local marketing opportunities, and how to convert online engagement into memberships.

The Result

Strategy they
could own.

The team implemented the strategy and saw real growth on Instagram. Better engagement, a growing local following, and content that actually reflected who they were as a gym. Not generic franchise posts, but something their community connected with.

What started as advice for one location expanded across multiple clubs as the management team grew within the franchise network. The strategy scaled because it was built to be repeatable, not dependent on us being in the room.

That's the goal of good advisory. Give people a system they can run with long after you've stepped back.
