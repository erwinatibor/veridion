Case Study

Selfie Snapshots

Getting a local business online when DIY just wasn't working.

Client

Selfie Snapshots

Services

Website Build

Industry

Photo Booth Hire

Live Site

selfiesnapshots.com.au

The Situation

Good business.
No website.

Selfie Snapshots had a solid local photo booth hire business but no functioning website. They'd tried to build one themselves — got lost in templates, settings, and options — and ended up with something that wasn't working for them.

They needed someone to take it off their hands. Set it up properly, make it look professional, and give them something they could actually send customers to.

The Approach

Simple, clean,
done right.

Not every project needs to be complex. Selfie Snapshots needed a clean, clear website that tells visitors what they offer, where they operate, and how to book. Nothing more, nothing less.

We took over the existing setup, cleaned it up, and shaped it into something that actually represents the business well. The right information in the right places, a design that doesn't get in the way, and a contact path that works.

The Result

Online.
Problem solved.

Selfie Snapshots went from a frustrating half-built DIY attempt to a clean, functional website they could be confident sending people to. For a local business, that's the whole job — make it easy for customers to find you, understand what you do, and reach out.

Sometimes the win is simple. A small local business that finally has a website that works.
